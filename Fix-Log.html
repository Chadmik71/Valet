9 August 2026

Checked if more customers would slow things down or cost more data Fixed

What was asked
If we get 10,000 customers, or 100 people a day using the hands-free shopping service with photos, will that use a lot more internet data?
What we found and fixed
More customers alone is fine — that part was already built well. But photos (of shopping bags or cars) were being saved in a way that made each record much bigger than normal — like putting a full photo inside a text message instead of sending a link to it. If we start taking lots of photos every day, this would have slowly used up a lot more internet data. We fixed it: photos are now stored in their own separate place, and the record just keeps a small link to it. This keeps things fast and cheap no matter how many photos we take.
9 August 2026

Fixed old reports possibly missing information Fixed

What was asked
Fix the issue where reports for older dates might not show everything.
What we found and fixed
The app only keeps the most recent 2,000 cars ready to use on each device — older ones get quietly dropped to make room. If someone downloaded a report for an older date after we passed 2,000 total cars, it could have shown incomplete numbers with no warning. We fixed it: reports now double check they have everything for the dates you ask for, and if something's missing, they go get it before building the report. Nothing changed about how fast the everyday app feels.

Fixed the printer not working for the first customer of the day Fixed

What was asked
On the first customer check-in of the day, the key tag didn't print by itself — had to press the print button by hand. Felt like "the printer was cold".
What we found and fixed
This exact problem happened before with a different printer type (Zebra), and was already fixed for that one — but the same fix was never added for your printer (BIXOLON). The very first connection to the printer each day needs a moment to "wake up" and get permission from the browser, and sometimes the first print job was sent before that was ready, so it silently failed. We added the same wake-up step for your printer too: the app now quietly checks the printer connection is ready in the background, before the first real print job needs it. No more manual button press needed.
